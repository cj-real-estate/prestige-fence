04 · Built Right

Engineered for Oklahoma weather.

Wind, clay soil, and hard sun are brutal on a fence. Ours are built for it — from the footing up.

  • I.

    Steel posts, set in concrete

    Galvanized steel posts resist leaning and rot far beyond wood-on-dirt construction.

  • II.

    Cedar kickboards

    A rot board at ground level takes the soil contact so pickets stay dry and last longer.

  • III.

    Braced corners & gate openings

    H-braces and corner assemblies keep tensioned lines plumb and tight for years.

  • IV.

    Clean sites, every day

    Old fence hauled off, debris cleared, and a final walkthrough before we call it done.
